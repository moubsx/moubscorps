# MatchPulse Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

MatchPulse collects the following types of data:

- **Device Advertising ID (GAID)**: Used to serve relevant ads via Google AdMob. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
- **Approximate Location**: Not collected. We do not access your location.
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous app usage events sent to Firebase Analytics (screen views, button taps) to improve app performance. This data is not personally identifiable.
- **Push Notification Token**: Firebase Cloud Messaging token to deliver match alerts for your favourite teams. This is not linked to personal identity.
- **Preferences**: Your selected teams, leagues, and theme preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded to any server.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

- Name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
- Precise GPS location
- Contacts, photos, or other device files
- Payment information

## 3. Data Sharing

We share data with the following third parties:

| Partner | Purpose | Policy |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Google AdMob | In-app advertising | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Firebase (Google) | Analytics & push notifications | [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| API-Football | Live match data (no user data sent) | — |
| Public RSS Feeds | News aggregation (no user data sent) | — |

We do not sell your data to any third parties.

## 4. Data Retention

- Ad ID data is managed by Google AdMob per their policies.
- Firebase analytics are retained for 14 months by default.
- Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you uninstall the app.

## 5. Children's Privacy

MatchPulse is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Your Rights

You may reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings under Privacy > Ads. To request deletion of any analytics data, contact us at the address below.
